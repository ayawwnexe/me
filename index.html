02 / so, who am I?

So, who am I?

I'm Ayan, a teenager with opinions on pretty much everything. I'm also aware that having an opinion doesn't mean I'm right, even if I sometimes act like it does.

I'm not the best at meeting new people. Sometimes I struggle to find the right words or end up making a simple conversation awkward. It's not that I don't want to talk to people. I just need a little time to get comfortable. Once I know you, though, getting me to shut up might be a different problem.

I like to think I'm a good listener. People sometimes come to me for advice, which is nice. Whether I can follow my own advice is a completely different question.

03 / interests and other nerdy stuff

Interests and other nerdy stuff

I like movies, TV shows, anime, manga and books. Basically, give me a story and there's a good chance I'll have something to say about it. I enjoy getting into the characters, thinking about what happened and occasionally talking way too much about whether an ending was actually good.

I also like making things. Websites, little experiments, random ideas that somehow turn into actual projects. There's something satisfying about having an idea, working on it and eventually seeing it come to life. It doesn't have to be anything huge. I just like making things of my own.

04 / what I want to do

What I want to do

I'm interested in forensic science as a career. I've always liked science, and the idea of using it to solve crimes is something I find genuinely interesting. Analysing evidence, figuring out what happened and relying on facts instead of just guessing sounds like something I'd enjoy doing.

And yes, Dexter probably has something to do with why I think solving crimes is cool. But the interest in science is real, I promise.

I'm still a student, so I've got a lot to learn before I get anywhere near that career. For now, I'm just interested in learning more about it and seeing where it takes me.

05 / a few things about me

A few things about me

Honesty matters a lot to me. I appreciate people who make an effort without having to be asked, and I don't really get along with people who are deliberately rude. I don't think being nice to someone should be that difficult.

I'm also an overthinker. I can spend ages stuck in my own head, and sometimes I get a little too confident in my own understanding of things. I know I don't have everything figured out, even if I occasionally forget that.
